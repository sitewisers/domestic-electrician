What Lee does

Most people ring about one of these four.

When the board keeps tripping

Fuse board upgrades

An old board with rewireable fuses, or one with no RCD protection, gets replaced with a modern consumer unit — then tested and certified. If something on the circuits is causing the trips, that gets found first rather than buried behind new kit.

When the wiring is past it

Full and partial rewires

Whole-house rewires, and partial ones. Plenty of houses only need the circuits that have had it replaced rather than the lot, so it is worth having someone look before you assume the worst.

For a sale, a mortgage or a tenancy

EICRs and remedial work

An Electrical Installation Condition Report puts the state of your wiring in writing. If it comes back unsatisfactory, the remedial work and the retest are handled here — you are not left ringing round for someone else to finish it.

When something has stopped working

Fault-finding and emergency callouts

Sockets dead down one side of the house. Lights out in one room. A breaker that will not stay in. Fault-finding is its own job: the point is locating the actual cause, not swapping parts until the symptom goes away.

And the rest of it

Cookers, ovens and appliances

Wired in on a circuit rated for what you have actually bought.

Lighting, inside and out

Indoor lighting, track lighting, and garden lighting wired to survive being outdoors.

Sockets and switches

New sockets where you need them, and switches moved, added or replaced.

Design, installation and testing

An installation designed from scratch, put in, then tested and inspected.

Certification

NICEIC Approved

NICEIC assess electrical contractors against BS 7671, the UK wiring regulations, and re-assess them to keep that approval in place.

What it means for you: the work has been checked by somebody other than the person who did it, and the certificates you are handed at the end are worth something.
